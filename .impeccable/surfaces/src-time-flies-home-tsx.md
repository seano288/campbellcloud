# Surface brief: Time Flies Home

Scope: the Home screen of Time Flies (src/time-flies/Home.tsx), with the cockpit language reaching the Round bar, end-of-Round, Hangar HUD and Trophy Shelf as light touches. Mode: Experience (a game's own menu; he is inside the game).

Audience and job: an early reader (6–7) on an iPad, sometimes an iPhone. On Home he starts a Round, visits his plane, or checks his Level in each kind of question. Constraint from the user: Play must never be hard to find. Keep the family look shared with Monster Truck Money.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Home is the cockpit of his plane, and the clock is its main instrument: tapping the clock gauge is Play. It refuses the incumbent recoloured tile grid, where Play was one tile among three and the Hangar tile outweighed it.

OWN-WORLD: a windshield strip of daytime sky above a gunmetal instrument panel (the clock bezel's #3b424c to #1c2027 metal, screws at the corners) with engraved white placards in Bungee. Amber annunciator lamps show Levels. Family traits stay: 4px black outlines, chunky offset shadows, Nunito for reading.

STORY: he understands that this is his plane's cockpit and the clock flies it; he believes he is the pilot; he taps the big clock to fly a Round.

FIRST VIEWPORT: portrait — status bar; sky windshield with TIME FLIES (about 16% of the height); panel below; the clock gauge in the centre at about 62% of the width with a green ▶ PLAY placard; a smaller round Hangar porthole showing his plane at the lower right; the Levels annunciator, 2×3, across the bottom. Landscape — windshield across the top; panel with Levels on the left, the clock gauge in the centre (largest), the Hangar on the right.

FORM: cockpit instrument panel; user-pinned, no roll. Authority: the user's own words ("Add a cockpit feel: rework Home around instruments and flying, with the clock as the hero"). Signature interaction: tapping the gauge spins the hands forward ("time flies") for about half a second before the Round starts; with Reduce Motion it starts at once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
