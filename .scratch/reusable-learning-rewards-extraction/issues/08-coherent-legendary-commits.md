# 08: Commit Legendary ownership and fitting together

**What to build:** An exact practice-money payment in the current checkout unlocks and fits its Legendary in one committed action, or changes nothing. Closing or completing the checkout prevents stale or repeated payment callbacks from granting or fitting anything.

**Blocked by:** 05, Load and save module-owned state compatibly.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Category:** enhancement

**Design basis:** Reusable learning, currency, and rewards design decisions 04, Legendary learning connection; 06, Save compatibility and isolation; and 08, Extraction evidence and handoff.

- [ ] The existing checkout uses an app-owned active identity and bound purchase action for its item and captured pricing context. Exact-payment results are inputs to coordination, not authority to grant a Mod or substitute another item/context.
- [ ] Against the latest Garage state, revalidate ownership, top-Rung prerequisite and the checked amount against Garage's price for the captured context. Empty, short, over, stale or rejected payments publish/save nothing and produce no success presentation.
- [ ] Acceptance synchronously publishes proposed ownership, immediate fitting and terminal checkout state together, then saves one whole aggregate and presents the committed purchase. For example, eligible Tires at the second context accept 110 cents and unlock/fit once.
- [ ] A closed, replaced, completed or unknown checkout cannot commit. Repeated success callbacks cannot grant/refit; an already-owned Legendary still fits freely through the ordinary owned-item action.
- [ ] Exact payment, rejection, closing and repeated callbacks leave currency, learning progression and all Trophy state unchanged. Checkout is neither a Round nor a zero-cost earned-currency purchase.
- [ ] A calculation failure before publication changes/saves nothing. A failed write after acceptance retains ownership, fit and consumed guard in memory; duplicate payment does not retry the grant, later successful actions save current state, and restart uses the last successful snapshot.
- [ ] Remove the live split unlock/fit path and route current checkout through this coordinator without also replacing asynchronous payment/help wiring. Ticket 12 owns scoped teaching, captured bank presentation and full checkout-lifetime replacement.
- [ ] Red-green tests at the approved Garage-proposal and app-checkout/persistence seams observe aggregate state, saved snapshots and checkout status, not private guard collections. Cover current-state eligibility changes, context substitution, duplicate/late callbacks, failures and unchanged non-Garage state. Focused tests/typechecking pass and existing CI gates remain green.

**Out of scope:** New Legendary policies, prices or Level gates; currency payment; full asynchronous checkout replacement; durable receipts; deployment or real player saves.
