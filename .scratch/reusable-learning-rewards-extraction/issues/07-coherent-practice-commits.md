# 07: Commit answer progress and Round earnings exactly once

**What to build:** Existing practice immediately retains earned learning progress, while a completed Round credits Bolts and Trophy completion effects once. Leaving, stale callbacks or a failed save cannot create duplicate rewards or undo already committed answers.

**Blocked by:** 05, Load and save module-owned state compatibly.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Category:** enhancement

**Design basis:** Reusable learning, currency, and rewards design decisions 02, Learning/Round interface; 03, Currency, purchases and ownership; 06, Save compatibility and isolation; and 08, Extraction evidence and handoff.

- [ ] App coordination owns the live routes for accepted miss/help facts, solved results and terminal Round facts. Bridge the current scheduler to these operations with stable session-local Round identity; do not replace its scheduling or all views in this ticket.
- [ ] Accepted miss/help breaks the streak immediately, even if the player leaves without solving. A solved result commits subject progression and streak effects together once before its success delay, using current progression and captured practice context. Unfinished problems do not enter the Mastery window.
- [ ] Starting practice, clearing its fresh tile and acknowledging introductions retain their existing saved effects. Abandonment keeps previously committed learning/streak state and introduces no invented solved result.
- [ ] Completion is accepted only for the active, nonterminal Round after its final delay. Currency calculates credit using the unchanged app policy and Trophies proposes completion effects; publish both and consume the terminal guard as one synchronous operation before showing success.
- [ ] Five clean answers credit six Bolts, two clean credit three and no clean answers credit one. Leaving during the final 900 ms delay, even after five solved results, abandons with no earnings or Round-completion effects. Completion and abandonment are mutually exclusive.
- [ ] Duplicate, unknown, inactive and replaced-Round terminal facts change neither currency nor Trophy state. Re-showing results cannot credit/count again, and a new Round remains eligible for its own completion.
- [ ] Invalid earnings, overflow or a pre-publication calculation failure publishes/saves none of the completion effects and leaves its guard unconsumed. Previously committed answer effects remain.
- [ ] A failed completion write retains in-memory credit, Trophy effects and the consumed guard. A duplicate still does nothing, a later successful action saves the full current aggregate, and restart reflects only the last successful snapshot. Guards are transient and no old terminal fact replays on load.
- [ ] Existing money progression, perfect/streak/milestone/day/counting-Trophy rules and dates remain real production rules, not prototype counters. End presentation consumes committed facts rather than applying rules itself.
- [ ] Replace legacy answer/completion writers as these operations become live. Red-green tests at the approved coordination/persistence seams observe public facts, published state and saved snapshots, including delayed abandonment, duplicate/stale facts, rejected calculations and failed writes. Focused tests/typechecking pass and existing CI gates remain green.

**Out of scope:** The shared Round controller replacement, new earnings/Trophy policy, durable delivery across restarts, receipt ledgers, redesigned celebrations or real player saves.
