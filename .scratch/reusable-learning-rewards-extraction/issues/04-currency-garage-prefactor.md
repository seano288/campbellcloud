# 04: Prefactor currency, Garage purchases and Goals

**What to build:** Garage purchase and Goal behaviour remains usable while currency arithmetic and Garage-owned rules become independent, testable proposals behind app compatibility adapters. Another reward can use the same currency contract without adopting Garage ownership or fitting.

**Blocked by:** 01, Capture automated baseline and synthetic save expectations.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Category:** enhancement

**Design basis:** Reusable learning, currency, and rewards design decisions 01, Module responsibilities; 03, Currency, purchases and ownership; 04, Legendary learning connection; 05, Presentation composition; and 08, Extraction evidence and handoff.

- [ ] Currency owns only the earned balance and pure credit/debit proposals using app-supplied earnings policy and subject-independent completed-Round facts. It imports no concrete subject, Round controller, Garage catalogue, branding or aggregate save type.
- [ ] Balances and amounts are non-negative safe integers, including zero. Reject fractions, negatives, nonnumbers, infinity, unsafe integers, insufficient funds and credit overflow without mutating inputs or returning partial replacement state.
- [ ] Garage owns catalogue, prices, eligibility, ownership, per-Body fits, Body/Colour selection and selected Goal. Its state/validators and rules accept a supplied balance or explicit pricing/payment context rather than reading another module through the global saved game.
- [ ] Pure Bolt-purchase proposals return Garage-owned rejection or proposed Garage state and cost. They include immediate fit, Colour application/Plain fallback, Body switch and selected-Goal clearing where the existing action requires them. Free owned-item actions remain distinct from purchases.
- [ ] Preserve Bolt prices of 5/15/30 per Rung, 30 for a bought Body and 3 for a bought Colour, with free starters/Red/defaults and no new Bolt-Rung prerequisites. Direct purchase of an owned item rejects.
- [ ] Preserve selected/automatic Goal rules, catalogue tie-breaking, Colour-only automatic fallback, exclusion of Legendaries and the separate current/newly-affordable queries. Compare before/after balances against the same captured reward state for newly-affordable facts.
- [ ] Garage Legendary eligibility, price tables and purchase proposals accept explicit Garage pricing context and checked practice-money amount, not a learning Level type or denomination set. Preserve top-Rung prerequisites and unchanged cash prices. Checkout remains separate from currency.
- [ ] Current purchase, fitting and Goal callers continue through app-owned compatibility adapters with unchanged saved fields and screens. This prefactor establishes rules; tickets 06 and 08 replace the existing multi-update commit paths.
- [ ] A minimal repeat-purchase reward accepts two intentional purchases through the same proposal/currency interfaces without a universal owned-item list, fitting model or one-time-unlock assumption. This does not specify recruitment mechanics.
- [ ] Red-green tests exercise returned currency/proposal/query results at approved seams, including invalid costs, stale affordability and rejected proposals. Focused tests and typechecking pass, existing CI gates stay green, and superseded rules have no remaining live callers.

**Out of scope:** Rebalancing, new Garage content, production army mechanics, alternative Legendary policies, save-format changes or full app coordination. Stop affected work if a new design conflict needs a decision.
