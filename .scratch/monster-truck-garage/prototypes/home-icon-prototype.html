Our icon is the dashed one (once in the grid, once in the dock). The label box is 100 pt wide at 12 pt, close to iPadOS; the real cut-off is checked by adding this page to the Home Screen.

Sizes to ship

iOS masks the apple-touch-icon into its rounded square itself, so the file is a full square with no transparency (see-through parts turn black). The maskable icon keeps everything inside the pink circle, the 80% safe zone, because Android and other launchers can cut it to a circle.

Try it on the iPad

Open this page in Safari on the iPad, pick a variant and a name, then Share → Add to Home Screen. The icon and the name that land on the Home Screen are the current ones. Add two or three to compare them side by side, then delete them.
