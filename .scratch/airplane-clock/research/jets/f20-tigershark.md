# Northrop F-20 Tigershark (research for hangar game)

Short note; geometry beyond the overall dimensions is estimated from photos and three-views.

- Length 14.4 m (47 ft 4 in; some sources 46 ft 6 in), span 8.13 m (26 ft 8 in; 8.53 m with tip missiles), height 4.2 m (13 ft 10 in). [MW]
- F-5E development with one GE F404 instead of two J85s; flattened "shark nose"; enlarged leading-edge extensions.
- Three prototypes built; never went into production.

[MW] https://military-history.fandom.com/wiki/F-20_Tigershark
[MF] https://migflug.com/?p=18384

## Shape pass 2026-11-13

No public-domain F-20 three-view found; the F-20 shares the F-5E's wing, tail, canopy and most of its body, so
planform and profile were measured from the F-5E three-view (pixel probe, ~98-103 px/m), with the F-20's nose, larger
LERX and single F404 from photos:
<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Northrop_F-5E_Tiger_II_3-view.svg>,
<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:F-20_Tigershark_at_California_Science_Center_Los_Angeles_right_side.JPG>

- Shark nose: flatter and wider than tall with sharp edges (superellipse n 1.6-1.8), a straight underside, short probe.
- Canopy raised and moved aft (windscreen 3.6, peak 5.3 m) flowing into the spine; body widens to 0.78 m half-width
  aft (one big engine).
- Wing moved aft to the F-5 position: LE 7.46 → 9.31 m (30°), root chord 2.9, tip 0.7, tip rails at z 4.1; low (y -0.25).
- LERX from the intake lips (x 5.45) out to the wing root (7.74, z 1.36). D intakes (lip x 5.5) under them.
- Low tailplane (11.8-13.3, y -0.2); fin with fillet from x 9.6, top 2.58 m, rudder TE leaning forward toward the top.
- Added the fairing over the nozzle seen in photos. Gear re-set for the 4.2 m height (main track ±1.55 m).
- Fin leading edges kept under ~58° sweep per segment (the builder's loft turns a steeper surface's thickness vertical, flattening it), with the low fillet ahead of the fin as a thin lofted body.
