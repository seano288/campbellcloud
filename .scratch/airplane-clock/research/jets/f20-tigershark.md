# Northrop F-20 Tigershark (research for hangar game)

Short note; geometry beyond the overall dimensions is estimated from photos and three-views.

- Length 14.4 m (47 ft 4 in; some sources 46 ft 6 in), span 8.13 m (26 ft 8 in; 8.53 m with tip missiles), height 4.2 m (13 ft 10 in). [MW]
- F-5E development with one GE F404 instead of two J85s; flattened "shark nose"; enlarged leading-edge extensions.
- Three prototypes built; never went into production.
