# Douglas A-4 Skyhawk (research for hangar game)

Short note; geometry beyond the overall dimensions is estimated from photos and three-views.

- A-4F: length 12.22 m (40 ft 1 in), span 8.38 m (27 ft 6 in), height 4.57 m (15 ft). Sources vary by about a foot on length. [DS][FL]
- Delta wing small enough not to need folding for carrier lifts; intakes beside the cockpit; A-4F avionics hump; tailplane set on the fin.
- Blue Angels flew the A-4F from 1974 to 1986.

[DS] https://www.dauntless-soft.com/MetalWings/HA1409.asp
[FL] https://www.flyingleathernecks.org/aircraft-collection/a-4f-skyhawk

## Shape pass 2026-11-13

Measured the spec against the A-4E three-view (pixel probe of the outline, ~100 px/m; the A-4F adds the hump):
<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Douglas_A-4E_Skyhawk_3-view_line_drawing.svg>

- Fuselage was too thin: now 1.54 m deep (spine +0.83, flat belly -0.71) with a drooping nose and straight underside,
  tapering up to the tailpipe (exit ~11.9 m, centre +0.3).
- Intakes were long separate tubes standing off the body; now D-shaped cheeks (lip x 3.85, outer half-width 0.83) that
  fade into the body by x 7, with small splitter plates.
- Wing: 43° leading edge from (4.35, z 0.55) to the tip (7.72, z 4.12), straight trailing edge at 8.56 m (was 9.4 → 8.9,
  too much chord); low on the body (y -0.5).
- Canopy now flows into the spine/hump (top only ~0.18 m above it); fin has the long dorsal fillet from x 7, swept LE and
  upright rudder (TE 12.1), top 3.05 m. Tailplane on the fin at +0.95.
- Added the starboard refuelling probe (to x -0.72). Gear re-set for the published 4.57 m height (belly clearance ~0.8 m,
  slight nose-up); hardpoints moved under the new wing.
- Fin leading edges kept under ~58° sweep per segment (the builder's loft turns a steeper surface's thickness vertical, flattening it), with the low fillet ahead of the fin as a thin lofted body.
