# McDonnell Douglas AV-8B Harrier II (research for hangar game)

Short note; geometry beyond the overall dimensions is estimated from photos and three-views.

- Length 14.12 m (46 ft 4 in), span 9.25 m (30 ft 4 in), height 3.55 m (11 ft 8 in). [NAVAIR]
- One Pegasus turbofan, four swivelling nozzles (front pair cool fan air, rear pair hot exhaust); shoulder wing with marked anhedral (about 12°, est.), anhedral tailplane.
- Bicycle main gear on the centre line with outrigger wheels under the wings.

[NAVAIR] https://www.navair.navy.mil/product/AV-8B-Harrier
[ASW] https://aerospaceweb.org/aircraft/attack/av8

## Shape pass 2026-11-13

Planform from the AV-8B top-view silhouette, profile and front view from the (small) AV-8B three-view, both on

were scaled to fit it):

- Intakes: huge D intakes from x 3.5 (outer half-width 1.27-1.29) either side of the cockpit, ~1 m tall.
- Drooping nose under a high bubble canopy (peak 1.05 at x 3.0); fat engine bay (half-width 1.0), long tail cone to
  14.12 m. Wing on the shoulder (root +0.74) drooping 12° to the tips, small LERX; drooped tailplane (root 11.6, chord
  1.9); taller fin (top 2.45, was 1.85).
- Ventral strakes under the belly; outriggers moved in to the real ~5.2 m track (z 2.6); bicycle gear shortened so it
  sits low as the real one does (ground -1.07, belly clearance ~0.45 m).
- Fin leading edges kept under ~58° sweep per segment (the builder's loft turns a steeper surface's thickness vertical, flattening it), with the low fillet ahead of the fin as a thin lofted body.
