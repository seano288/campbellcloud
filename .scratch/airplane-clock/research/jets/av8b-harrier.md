# McDonnell Douglas AV-8B Harrier II (research for hangar game)

Short note; geometry beyond the overall dimensions is estimated from photos and three-views.

- Length 14.12 m (46 ft 4 in), span 9.25 m (30 ft 4 in), height 3.55 m (11 ft 8 in). [NAVAIR]
- One Pegasus turbofan, four swivelling nozzles (front pair cool fan air, rear pair hot exhaust); shoulder wing with marked anhedral (about 12°, est.), anhedral tailplane.
- Bicycle main gear on the centre line with outrigger wheels under the wings.
