# Grumman X-29 (research for hangar game)

Short note; geometry beyond the overall dimensions is estimated from photos and three-views.

- Length 14.66 m (48 ft 1 in) without the nose probe, span 8.29 m (27 ft 2 in), height about 4.36 m (14 ft 3.5 in; sources range to 14 ft 9 in). [WP]
- Wings swept forward more than 33° (quarter chord), close-coupled canards, F-5A forward fuselage, F-16 landing gear, one F404, aft strake flaps.
- Unstable by design: its flight computers made about 40 corrections a second.
- Not called "Polecat": that is a different, Lockheed unmanned aircraft (the P-175).

[WP] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grumman_X-29
[MF] https://migflug.com/?p=18382

## Shape pass 2026-11-13

Measured from the NASA line-art three-view on Wikimedia Commons, "Grumman X-29 3-view line art.svg"
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grumman_X-29_3-view_line_art.svg), scaled to its 48 ft 1 in length and 27 ft 2.44 in
span. What changed in `jets/x29.ts`:
- **Canards moved forward about 1.9 m** onto the intakes, beside the cockpit: root LE 5.4 m, tip LE 6.4 m at z 2.1 m (they
  were behind the canopy at 7.3 m).
- **Wing moved forward and reshaped.** Forward-swept leading edge from a gloved root (8.25 m at z 0.6 m, 8.7 m at z 1.6 m) to
  the tip at 7.3 m, z 4.14 m; tip chord 1.2 m; trailing edge swept forward about 44° to 11.4 m at the root.
- **Strakes** are now the drawing's long flat shelves along the aft fuselage, 11.2 to 14.0 m, out to z 1.14 m.
- **Fin smaller and further aft**: root LE 12.0 m, tip LE 13.9 m, tip chord 0.76 m (it was 3.2 m chord at 10.9 m).
- **Deeper fuselage**: belly about 0.9 m off the ground, spine 2.35 m, a 1.2 m-tall tailpipe (nozzle radius 0.5 m).
- **Lower canopy and pilot** (the helmet poked through the glass), a nose test probe, the nose gear moved forward to 3.2 m.
