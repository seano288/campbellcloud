# Grumman X-29 (research for hangar game)

Short note; geometry beyond the overall dimensions is estimated from photos and three-views.

- Length 14.66 m (48 ft 1 in) without the nose probe, span 8.29 m (27 ft 2 in), height about 4.36 m (14 ft 3.5 in; sources range to 14 ft 9 in). [WP]
- Wings swept forward more than 33° (quarter chord), close-coupled canards, F-5A forward fuselage, F-16 landing gear, one F404, aft strake flaps.
- Unstable by design: its flight computers made about 40 corrections a second.
- Not called "Polecat": that is a different, Lockheed unmanned aircraft (the P-175).
