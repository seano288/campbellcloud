# Glossary

**Game Mode**: One kind of money practice. There are three: Learn the Coins (names and values), Count the Cash (how much is here?) and Pay the Shop (pick the right money). They open in that order: each one after the previous reaches Level 2. The player picks which open Game Mode to play.
_Avoid_: Pay the Garage (it clashes with the Garage, which uses Bolts, not money)

**Level**: How hard a Game Mode is. Levels 1 to 3 are set by which money appears: Level 1 uses pennies, nickels and dimes, Level 2 adds quarters and Level 3 adds $1 and $5 bills. Levels 4 and 5 use all the money and ask for something harder: Learn the Coins asks which one piece is worth the same as a pile (piles of one kind, then mixed piles), Count the Cash counts bigger jumbled piles up to $9.99 and then the change from paying for something, and Pay the Shop pays prices up to $9.75 with the $5 bill and then pays with the fewest pieces. Each Game Mode has its own Level. The game raises it itself when the player shows mastery; nobody picks it by hand, and it never goes down.

**Stretch**: How big a problem is within its Level: smaller piles, lower prices and fewer answers to pick from while the player is new to the Level or missing a lot, growing as their first-try answers outnumber their misses.
_Avoid_: Level (a Stretch never changes the money or the kind of problem)

**Mastery**: Getting 8 of the last 10 problems in a Game Mode right on the first try without help, or the last 6 in a row. It raises that Game Mode's Level; at Level 5 it gives the star. Only problems asked at the player's Level count.

**Round**: A short run of problems in one Game Mode that ends in a reward. A problem he missed comes back once, later in the Round. Above Level 1 the first problem, and the one right after a miss or help, is a warm-up from the Level below that doesn't count toward Mastery.

**Bolt**: What the player earns in Rounds, one for each answer right on the first try without help plus one for finishing, and spends to unlock Mods and Colours and buy Bodies. It is deliberately *not* money, so spending it never asks the player to count cash. A Bolt never buys a Legendary Mod.
_Avoid_: Reward Token, coin, star, point

**Truck**: The player's one monster truck: a Body with Mods fitted to it. A player has one Truck at a time.

**Body**: The basic shape of the Truck. There are starter Bodies, which the player picks from on first launch and owns from the start, and Bodies bought with Bolts. The player can switch between owned Bodies at any time, and each Body remembers which Mods were fitted to it.
_Avoid_: Starter Truck, model

**Slot**: One place on the Truck that holds a single Mod at a time, such as Tires or Paint.
_Avoid_: Category, part type

**Mod**: A part, look or sound that goes in one Slot (tires, paint, flames, lights, an engine and so on). A Mod is unlocked once, by spending Bolts (or money, for a Legendary), and cooler Mods cost more. After that it can be fitted to any Body or taken off freely.

**Rung**: A Mod's step within its Slot: first, second, top, then Legendary. A higher Rung looks cooler and costs more. The first three are paid in Bolts; the Legendary Rung is paid in money, tapped into the Pay the Shop tray to the exact price, and opens for purchase once the top Rung in its Slot is unlocked. A Legendary's price follows the player's Pay the Shop Level (Levels 4 and 5 keep Level 3's prices) and uses only that Level's money, so it grows as he improves; no Level is needed to buy one. Each Slot also has a free default that is not a Rung.
_Avoid_: Tier, level (Level means how hard a Game Mode is)

**Door Number**: The racing number on the Truck's door, 0 to 99, which the player picks himself with two wheels (tens and ones) while it is read aloud. Each Body keeps its own number, and changing it is always free. It is not a Mod: the Door Number Slot's Mods are only its style (Plain, Outlined, Flaming, Glowing Gold), and with No Number fitted it isn't shown.

**Colour**: The base coat of one Body, such as Red, Teal or Sky Blue. There are twelve. Red is free and the rest cost 3 Bolts each, bought once and then used on any Body. Each Body keeps its own Colour. It is not a Mod and not a Slot: it sits alongside the Paint Slot and is picked from a row of swatches in the Paint sheet. It shows on Plain and Fire Fade (the fire fades into it); the other Paint Mods cover it.
_Avoid_: paint (Paint is the Slot), color

**Goal**: The one locked Mod, Body or Colour the player is saving Bolts toward, shown as a progress bar while they play. It is the cheapest Mod or Body unless the player taps another locked Mod, Body or Colour to choose it. A Colour becomes the Goal on its own only once nothing else bought with Bolts is left. A Legendary is never the Goal, because it is bought with money.

**Garage**: Where the Truck is built. It shows the Truck, the Mods already unlocked and the Mods still to unlock, each with its Bolt cost (or money price, for a Legendary), and the Bodies owned and still to buy.

**Show Off**: The Garage's stage moment: the current Truck spins under a spotlight while the crowd cheers, and performs a move when tapped.

**Trophy**: A reward earned by playing well: reaching a Level or a star, a streak of answers right on the first try, a perfect Round, or sticking with it (Rounds finished, days played). A Trophy is never bought and never lost. It isn't a Mod, it isn't bought with Bolts and it earns none. A counting Trophy upgrades in place, bronze to silver to gold and on, rather than taking a new spot. Trophies stand on the Trophy Shelf in the Garage, on three planks: Learning, Skill and Sticking with it. Earned ones shine and the rest are grey silhouettes.
_Avoid_: badge, achievement, award

## Time Flies

Time Flies reuses the terms above where they mean the same thing (Round, Level, Mastery, Bolt, Slot, Mod, Rung, Goal,
Trophy). These are its own:

**Game Modes (Time Flies)**: the six kinds of question. The clock: Find the Clock (hear a time, tap the clock that shows
it), Read the Clock (one clock, pick its time from three read aloud) and Set the Clock (drag the hands); Level 1 is
whole hours, Level 2 quarter hours and Level 3 every five minutes. The calendar: Days of the Week (tomorrow, then
yesterday, then two days on), Months of the Year (the month after, the month before, then "the third month") and Time
Facts (how many seconds in a minute, minutes in an hour, hours in a day...). Find the Clock, Days of the Week and Time
Facts are open from the start; Read the Clock opens when Find the Clock reaches Level 2, Set the Clock after Read the
Clock, and Months of the Year after Days of the Week. He never picks one: every Round mixes all the open ones.

**Mixed Round (Time Flies)**: each question is a different kind from the one before. Most are asked at that kind's
Level, to push him, and only those count toward Mastery; about one in three, and always the one right after a miss,
comes from an easier Level.

**Mastery (Time Flies)**: Getting 16 of the last 20 questions of one kind, asked at its Level, right on the first try
without help, twice the run Monster Truck Money asks for, so he stays on each Level longer.

**Aircraft**: one of the 18 planes in the Hangar, Time Flies' Body. Three starters (P-51 Mustang, F-16 Falcon, F/A-18
Super Hornet) are his from the start; the rest cost 30 Bolts.
_Avoid_: jet (the P-51, P-38 and Corsair have propellers), Body

**Hangar**: where he builds his Aircraft, Time Flies' Garage.

**Time Lock**: the vault that holds every Legendary in Time Flies. Once a Slot's top Rung is his, tapping its Legendary
asks him to set the clock to a time; the vault opens when he does. A Legendary is never bought with Bolts.

**Take Off**: the Hangar's stage moment, Time Flies' Show Off: the Aircraft rolls down a runway, lifts off and climbs
into the sky with its Smoke and Engine. Up there it does a trick when tapped, fires its guns while 🔫 is held, and each 🚀
tap launches the next store its Weapons Mod hangs under the wings (reloaded a few seconds later). 🛬 lands it back on
the runway and returns to the Hangar.

**Tail Number**: Time Flies' Door Number, painted on the fins.

## Chop Chop

Chop Chop reuses Time Flies' terms where they mean the same thing (Mixed Round, Mastery, Bolt, Slot, Mod, Rung, Goal,
Trophy, Tail Number). These are its own:

**Game Modes (Chop Chop)**: the seven kinds of fraction question: Fair Shares, Name the Piece, Bigger Piece, Find the
Part, Color the Part, Share It and Top and Bottom. Fair Shares, Find the Part and Share It are open from the start; Name
the Piece opens when Fair Shares reaches Level 2, Bigger Piece and Top and Bottom after Name the Piece, and Color the Part
after Find the Part. Mastery is 16 of the last 20, or 10 in a row.

**Part**: some of the equal pieces of a whole, said in words ("three quarters", "three of the four equal pieces"), and,
once he has met writing, also written as numbers.

**Top and Bottom**: a part written with numbers, the top number over the bottom number (3 over 4). The bottom number is
"how many equal pieces" and the top number is "how many pieces are left". It's always read aloud with the words he knows
("three quarters"), never as "three over four" or "three fourths". Writing is met once, before the first Top and Bottom
question; after that the numbers are shown beside the words everywhere.
_Avoid_: numerator, denominator (to him; fine in code comments)

**Helicopter**: one of the 17 on the Helipad, Chop Chop's Body. Three starters (UH-60 Black Hawk, MH-6 Little Bird,
AH-1Z Viper); the rest cost 30 Bolts.

**Helipad**: where he builds his Helicopter, Chop Chop's Garage.

**Fuel Lock**: the vault that holds every Legendary in Chop Chop. Once a Slot's top Rung is his, tapping its Legendary
asks him to fill the fuel tank to a part ("three quarters full"); it opens when he does.

**Lift Off**: the Helipad's stage moment: the rotors spin up, it lifts straight off the helipad and flies into the sky,
does a trick when tapped, fires its gun and rockets, and lands back on the pad.

## Sight Squad

Sight Squad reuses Chop Chop's terms where they mean the same thing (Mixed Round, Mastery, Bolt, Slot, Mod, Rung, Goal,
Trophy). These are its own:

**Sight word**: one of the 100 common words he learns (Dolch pre-primer and primer, Fry's first hundred), met one at a
time in a fixed order of 20 sets of 5. Look-alikes (was/saw, on/no, the/then) are never in the same set.
_Avoid_: vocabulary word, spelling word

**Heart word**: a sight word with letters that don't say what they usually say (the *ai* in *said*), its **heart part**.
Those letters get a heart when it's taught, to be learned "by heart"; the rest of the word is read the usual way.

**Meeting a word**: the moment a new word arrives, before any question uses it: it's shown big, said, spelled letter by
letter ("Say it, spell it, say it"), its heart part shown, used in a sentence, and then he taps it.

**Strength**: how well he knows one word, 0 to 5: up one on a first-try answer, down two on a miss. Weak and new words
come up more often. The next word waits while more than two are shaky (below 2).
_Avoid_: Level (a Level belongs to a kind of question, not a word)

**Game Modes (Sight Squad)**: the six kinds of question: Find It (hear a word, tap it), Word Hunt (tap every copy in a
grid), Build It (tap its letters in order), Read It (read a word, pick which of three spoken words it is), Sentence Hunt
(find the word in a sentence read to him) and Fill the Gap (pick the missing word of a sentence). Find It, Word Hunt and
Build It are open from the start; each opens the one below it on Home at Level 2.

**Army**: his toy soldiers, green plastic army men on a play-mat, Sight Squad's Body. It's many figures, not one.

**Squad**: one kind of figure in the Army (Rifleman, Scout, Drummer, Medic, Rocket Trooper, Army Dog, Paratrooper, Jeep,
Cannon, Tank). Riflemen are his from the start; the rest are unlocked once with Bolts, and the first soldier comes with it.
_Avoid_: unit type, class

**Recruit**: one more soldier of a Squad he has, bought with Bolts (2 to 12) and bought again as often as he likes, up to
the Squad's maximum. It marches onto the mat. Recruits are never the Goal.

**Gear**: the Mods the whole Army wears, one Rung per Slot: Helmet, Pack, Flag, Finish, Mat (the play-mat itself) and Boom
(what it throws in a Battle).

**Team Colour**: the one colour of plastic the whole Army is made of. Army Green, Tan and Grey are free (he picks one on
first launch); nine more cost 3 Bolts.
_Avoid_: paint, Colour (in Sight Squad it's always the Team Colour)

**Camp**: where he builds his Army, Sight Squad's Garage.

**Password Lock**: the vault that holds every Legendary in Sight Squad. Once a Slot's top Rung is his, tapping its
Legendary asks him to build a password, a word he has met, from letter tiles.

**Parade**: one of the Camp's two stage moments: the Army marches round the mat in a column, and does what the command words
he taps say (go, stop, run, jump, look, come, down, up, play: only the ones he has met, written only).

**Battle**: the other stage moment: a toy battle against wind-up robots. Robots that are hit topple over and bounce back up,
then go poof; his soldiers that are hit fall over and spring back up. He always wins. Never "kill", "shoot" or "destroy".
